Welcome to Mentamath 👋

What this is: a mental math trainer. Pick a mode — arithmetic, exponents, n choose r, divisibility, factoring, mixed, or your own combo — and answer as fast as you can.

Track your streak 🔥 and accuracy as you go. Three difficulty levels and four number ranges keep it fresh.

How it works:

• Type your answer — it auto-submits the moment it's correct, no Enter needed

• Divisibility? Just tap Yes ✓ or No ✗

• Get it wrong? No skipping — keep trying until you nail it

• Factoring? Type like 2^4×3 or 2x2x2x2x3
